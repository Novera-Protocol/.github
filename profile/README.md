# Novera Protocol

> **Infrastructure for Trust.**

Novera Protocol is a modular real-world-asset lifecycle platform being designed to make asset registration, identity status, registry records, deed-style evidence, and lifecycle visibility more coherent and auditable.

## Public focus

- **NovaID** — future identity and organization verification status
- **NovaRegistry** — future asset registration and lifecycle records
- **NovaDeed** — future evidence and deed-style record lifecycle
- **Compliance Engine** and **Legal API Gateway** — future policy and legal-integration boundaries

## Technology position

Novera is **EVM-first and chain-agnostic**. Future deployment environments will be selected against settlement assurance, operational risk, cost, ecosystem maturity, institutional compatibility, and interoperability needs. Hedera and Chainlink are potential future integration adapters where justified; no live blockchain or oracle integration is represented here.

## Current status

Novera is in a foundation and prototype-design stage. The public materials describe planned architecture and product direction only. All MVP demonstrations use synthetic data; no real identity verification, payment processing, custody, legal documents, token issuance, stablecoin issuance, or production deployment is live.
