# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Novera Protocol is committed to a professional, respectful, and constructive environment for everyone who engages with its public repositories and documentation.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate with professionalism, good faith, and respect.
- Keep public claims factual, attributable where appropriate, and consistent with Novera's pre-launch status.
- Protect confidential information, personal data, credentials, and security-sensitive material.
- Accept thoughtful review, disagreement, and correction without harassment or retaliation.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate misinformation, impersonation, disclosure of private information, spam, and attempts to introduce credentials, private keys, customer data, legal documents, or malicious content are not acceptable.

## Scope

This Code applies to public issues, pull requests, discussions, documentation, and other Novera Protocol community spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, limit participation, or take other proportionate action to protect the integrity, safety, and professional standards of Novera Protocol's public spaces.
